What is an active cell-day & unknown coverage?

Active cell-day: One H3 resolution-8 grid cell (~0.74 km²) with at least one quality-filtered vegetation detection (MODIS ≥ 30 or VIIRS n/h, type = 0) in a UTC day. It combines satellite passes into one daily unit — not distinct fires or burned area.

Raw detections: All CSV rows across satellites, confidence levels, and source types before merging overlaps.

Unknown coverage: Cloud and overpass masks are unavailable in these CSVs. Zero means no qualifying detections were recorded, not confirmed absence of fire.
